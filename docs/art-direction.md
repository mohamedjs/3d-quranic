# Art direction — from the "Quran Journey" concept board

Target look: warm animated-film (stylized, not photoreal) · golden hour · lush Nile-village greens
against warm clay · UI = dark teal glass + thin gold line + soft glow · type = Tajawal/Nunito, Amiri Quran.

## Shape language
- Round, soft silhouettes; chunky readable forms; no thin hard edges.
- Characters: big head (~1:5 child, 1:6.5 adult), large warm eyes, rim-lit skin.
- UI: 16 px radii, pill buttons, 1 px gold line + faint outer glow, numbered choice chips,
  circular badges (objective "!", medal, compass). Icons: filled gold glyphs, 2 px outline style.
- Illustrations: 4–6 depth layers, strong atmospheric perspective (far = pale/blue, near = dark/warm),
  backlit sun, dust haze, rim light on silhouettes, vignette.
